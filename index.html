PixelFlow 2.0

Help and shortcuts

Move the pointer over the canvas to interact. Every mode has its own sliders and presets in the right panel.

Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z redo · R randomize · F fullscreen · S save · E export PNG · Space pause

Particle Playground: click to cause an explosion. Fractal Explorer: scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to reset the view. PhotoFlow: upload a photo and the particles rebuild it; move the pointer to scatter them.
